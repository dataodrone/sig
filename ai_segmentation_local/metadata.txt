about=
    AI Segmentation Local is a fully local, no-cost alternative for object segmentation in QGIS.

    - Automatic mode: draw a zone on your imagery, type what you are looking for ("buildings", "trees", "solar panels") and get every match back as vector polygons with a confidence value.
    - Semi-auto mode: click one object on the map, optionally add negative points (right click), and get a clean outline.

    Everything runs on your own machine with open-weight models (SAM + CLIP), downloaded once on first run. No account, no API key, no subscription, no credits, and nothing is ever sent over the network. Works on CPU, no GPU needed.

    Works on any raster layer: GeoTIFF, JPG, PNG, and online basemaps (WMS, XYZ tiles).
